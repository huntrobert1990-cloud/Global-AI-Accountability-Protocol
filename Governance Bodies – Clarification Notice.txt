Governance Bodies – Clarification Notice
Conceptual Oversight Entities
Certain governance bodies referenced within this framework — including the IAEB (International AI Ethics Board) and related oversight structures — are conceptual entities proposed within the GAAP model.
They are not currently established legal organizations, nor do they represent existing institutions, regulatory authorities, or formal alliances.
These entities are included to illustrate how a structured, multi-layered oversight system could function if adopted at national or international levels.
No claim of affiliation, endorsement, or operational existence is implied.
Intent of Inclusion
The purpose of these proposed bodies is to:
Demonstrate institutional architecture for accountability
Provide structural models for certification and escalation
Offer a blueprint for future implementation
They serve as framework components, not active governing authorities.
Ongoing Revision
Future versions of GAAP may:
Replace conceptual bodies with references to existing institutions
Refine their structure
Remove them entirely based on community critique
