## 🎯 Overview

The Global AI Accountability Protocol (GAAP) 2.2 is a secular, evidence-based framework designed to ensure ethical, transparent, and accountable AI systems worldwide. Released in July 2025, GAAP 2.2 addresses emerging challenges including neuro-rights, environmental sustainability, bias mitigation, whistleblower protections, and governance interoperability.

**Current Status:** Active Standard | Certified for Deployment

---

## 🤔 What is GAAP?

GAAP provides:

- **Universal Standards** applicable regardless of cultural, religious, or philosophical backgrounds
- **Tiered Requirements** scaling from low-impact local systems to critical military applications
- **Enforceable Mechanisms** with clear penalties and accountability pathways
- **Cross-Jurisdictional Compatibility** harmonizing with GDPR, CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA, and international standards
- **Practical Implementation Guidance** with detailed checklists and certification processes

### Who Should Use GAAP?

- **Technology Companies** developing AI products and services
- **Healthcare Institutions** deploying AI for diagnosis, treatment, or patient management
- **Financial Services** using AI for risk assessment, fraud detection, or customer service
- **Government Agencies** implementing AI in public services or critical infrastructure
- **Research Institutions** advancing AI capabilities
- **Any Organization** deploying AI systems that affect human welfare, rights, or opportunities

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛡️ Comprehensive Protection Framework

- **10 Core Principles** covering transparency, fairness, safety, data sovereignty, and more
- **Neuro-Rights Protections** for brain-computer interfaces and cognitive liberty
- **Indigenous Data Sovereignty** with OCAP® compliance requirements
- **Environmental Accountability** with mandatory carbon impact disclosure

### 📊 Risk-Based Tiering

- **Tier 1:** Local, Low Impact (simplified requirements)
- **Tier 2:** Organizational, Moderate Risk (external oversight required)
- **Tier 3:** National, High-Risk (comprehensive demographic validation)
- **Tier 4:** Military, Governmental, Critical (maximum safeguards)

### 🔒 Strong Enforcement

- **72-Hour Whistleblower Escalation** with automatic external notification
- **Immutable Audit Trails** using cryptographic security
- **Public Challenge Pathways** with binding ombudsman decisions
- **Graduated Penalties** from warnings to criminal prosecution

### 🌍 Global Interoperability

- Cross-certification with major regulatory frameworks
- Unified audit criteria reducing compliance burden
- Recognition agreements with 50+ jurisdictions
- Compatible with ISO/IEC and IEEE standards

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Determine Your Tier

Classify your AI system based on:
- **Impact Scope:** Number of affected users
- **Decision Autonomy:** Level of human oversight
- **Data Sensitivity:** Types of personal data processed

**Quick Classification:**
- <1,000 users, low data sensitivity, high oversight → **Tier 1**
- 1,000-100,000 users, moderate data/autonomy → **Tier 2**
- >100,000 users OR sensitive data OR low oversight → **Tier 3**
- Critical infrastructure OR military/governmental → **Tier 4**

### 2. Review Requirements

Read the relevant sections in the White Paper:
- **All Tiers:** Section 3 (Core Principles)
- **Your Tier:** Section 4.X (Implementation Tiers)
- **Enforcement:** Section 5 (Enforcement Mechanisms)

### 3. Gap Analysis

Compare your current practices against GAAP requirements:
- Review Appendix E (Certification Checklist)
- Identify missing safeguards
- Prioritize implementation

### 4. Implementation

Follow the implementation guidance:
- Establish required infrastructure (audit logs, explainability tools, etc.)
- Appoint Responsible AI Officer (Tier 2+)
- Set up whistleblower portal (Tier 2+)
- Conduct bias testing and documentation

### 5. Certification

- Complete documentation requirements
- Engage GAAP-accredited auditor
- Undergo certification audit
- Receive certification and begin deployment

## 📊 Implementation Tiers

### Tier 1: Local, Low Impact

**Examples:** Internal productivity tools, personal assistants, entertainment recommendation

**Key Requirements:**
- Basic transparency and explainability
- Fairness testing before deployment
- Hybrid human escalation on distress
- Regional lexicon integration

**Certification:** Self-certification permitted

---

### Tier 2: Organizational

**Examples:** HR screening, customer service chatbots, fraud detection, content moderation

**Key Requirements:**
- External explainability tools
- Proxy variable audits
- Whistleblower escalation pathway
- Responsible AI Officer (RAIO)
- Immutable audit logging

**Certification:** Third-party audit required (valid 2 years)

---

### Tier 3: National, High-Risk

**Examples:** Healthcare diagnostics, credit scoring, criminal justice risk assessment, critical infrastructure

**Key Requirements:**
- Demographic accuracy validation
- Confidence-accuracy divergence auto-review
- Indigenous ethical review (where applicable)
- Cross-border data consent
- Public ombudsman access

**Certification:** Comprehensive audit required (valid 18 months)

---

### Tier 4: Military, Governmental, Critical

**Examples:** Autonomous weapons, national security systems, life-support systems, emergency response

**Key Requirements:**
- Neuro-pain data recognition
- Absolute human veto on overrides
- Global tribunal transparency
- Neural lag verification
- Continuous monitoring

**Certification:** Rigorous international audit (valid 12 months, quarterly reviews)

## 🎯 Core Principles

GAAP 2.2 is founded on 10 core principles:

1. **Transparency & Explainability** - Auditable outputs with user-accessible explanations
2. **Fairness & Bias Mitigation** - Rigorous testing across demographic groups
3. **Safety & Harm Minimization** - Real-time detection with contextual assessment
4. **Data Sovereignty & Governance** - User ownership and control of data
5. **Environmental Sustainability** - Lifecycle carbon impact disclosure
6. **Neuro-Rights & Cognitive Liberty** - Protection for brain-computer interfaces
7. **Whistleblower Protections** - 72-hour escalation with anti-retaliation measures
8. **Accountability & Oversight** - Independent RAIOs and immutable audit logs
9. **Public Right to Challenge** - Binding ombudsman review pathways
10. **Global Interadoptability** - Cross-certification with major frameworks

*See White Paper Section 3 for detailed implementation guidance.*

## 🏁 Getting Started

### For Organizations

**Step 1: Assessment**
```bash
# Download the tier classification tool
wget https://gaap.org/tools/tier-classifier.py
python tier-classifier.py --system-description "your AI system"
```

**Step 2: Documentation**
- Review required documentation (Appendix E)
- Collect training data provenance
- Document model architecture
- Prepare safety feature descriptions

**Step 3: Implementation**
- Set up required infrastructure
- Appoint RAIO (if Tier 2+)
- Implement audit logging
- Create whistleblower portal (if Tier 2+)

**Step 4: Certification**
- Contact GAAP-accredited auditor
- Schedule certification audit
- Complete remediation if needed
- Receive certification

## 🎓 Certification

### Certification Process

1. **Application** - Submit system documentation
2. **Audit** - Third-party evaluation by accredited auditor
3. **Remediation** - Address identified deficiencies
4. **Certification** - Receive GAAP certificate and certification mark
5. **Monitoring** - Ongoing compliance reporting

### Certification Bodies

Find GAAP-accredited certification bodies in your region:

**North America:**
- United States: AI Safety Commission (ASC)
- Canada: Office of the AI Ombudsman
- Mexico: Autoridad de Inteligencia Artificial Responsable

**Europe:**
- European Union: EU AI Ethics Board
- United Kingdom: Centre for Data Ethics and Innovation (CDEI)
- Switzerland: Federal AI Accountability Office

**Asia-Pacific:**
- Singapore: AI Governance Committee
- Australia: Responsible AI Framework Authority
- Japan: AI Principles Implementation Office

*Full list available at [ieab-gaap.org/regional-authorities]*

### Certification Costs

Certification costs vary by tier and system complexity:

| Tier | Typical Audit Cost | Certification Validity |
|------|-------------------|----------------------|
| Tier 1 | Self-certification (free) | Ongoing |
| Tier 2 | $10,000 - $50,000 | 2 years |
| Tier 3 | $50,000 - $250,000 | 18 months |
| Tier 4 | $250,000+ | 12 months |

*Costs are estimates and vary by auditor, system complexity, and organization size.*
